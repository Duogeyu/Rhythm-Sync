## 2026-03-19 - Replacing Fuse.js with fuzzysort for backend performance
**Learning:** `fuzzysort` is preferred over `Fuse.js` for performance optimizations in string matching across the codebase. When replacing `Fuse.js` with `fuzzysort` in multi-field searches (like title and artist), each field must be explicitly prepared and mapped in the `keys` array (e.g. `['preparedTitle', 'preparedArtist']`) to prevent secondary fields from being ignored. The native negative scoring from `fuzzysort` must also be normalized to a 0-1 scale using `Math.max(0, (score + 1000) / 1000)` to remain compatible with legacy ranking logic, and a threshold of `-300` corresponds to the legacy `fuse.js` threshold of `0.3`.
**Action:** When making fuzzy matching changes in the backend, always ensure the `.jules` directory exists before writing logs, explicitly prepare and map multiple fields in `fuzzysort`, and ensure score mapping is correct to maintain backward compatibility.
